# Offer equipment coverage

The shared Asset Library includes the equipment named in offer VP207BNS202603,
issued 3 November 2026. Search by the item name or by `VP207BNS202603`.
The items can be placed through Quick Search or the symbol library, and use the
same geometry in Live 3D, the standalone preview and the 3D viewfinder.

The catalog includes Nanlite 2400B, Aputure 1200D with F10, the F10 attachment,
Aputure 600D, Amaran 300C, Amaran F22C, INFINIBAR PB12, white floppy, stand kit,
cable kit, 32 Amp and 63 Amp distribution boxes, grip kit, flag and diffusion
kit, apple box kit, Super wind up, Hi Hi roller and wind up stands, 20x20 Magic
Cloth, 12x12 and 8x8 UB fabric, grid cloth and frames, iPad, Black out, Cerise,
and a Renault Trafic transport van.

The geometry and equipment footprints are illustrative estimates. Manufacturer
dimensions and electrical loads remain unspecified. Model numbers are used as
names only. Named fabric and frame sizes are interpreted as feet and converted
exactly to millimetres. New placements use the current plan calibration.
**Reset to physical size** in the shape inspector reapplies the catalog footprint
to an existing placement. Cloth-only entries appear as fabric sheets,
and frames appear as open frames. Unspecified kits use labeled case symbols.
UB and Cerise retain the offer's wording. The control equipment uses generic
preview geometry. The transport van reuses the generic van model.

The supplier's prices, client details, banking information and rental terms
are outside the asset catalog. Rental quantities belong to the production's
equipment list and can be represented by repeated placements as required.

Named lighting variants reuse the standard fixture artwork in the search palette,
floor plan and print export. Placed objects use existing shape entities and
symbol IDs, including their normal save, undo, copy and collaboration paths.
