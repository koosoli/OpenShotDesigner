# Furniture geometry

The furniture and cabinet geometry in Live 3D is adapted from ConstructCAD
Components contributors under CC BY 4.0. The source families are bed, chair,
desk, nightstand, round table, rectangular table, sofa, bookcase, kitchen
base cabinet, wall cabinet and wardrobe. Cineplan converts
the source dimensions to millimetres, aligns the backs of seats with its
floor plan symbols, and scales each footprint to the placed asset.
